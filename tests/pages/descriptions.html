Deepak Subramani

DS 393 (JAN) 3:1 High-performance computing for Quantum Modeling of Materials

Phani Motamarri

Density functional theory, iterative eigenvalue algorithms, Krylov subspace methods and finite-element discretization on hybrid CPU-GPU architectures.

Prerequisites: DS 211 or background in Numerical Linear Algebra, and consent of instructor.
