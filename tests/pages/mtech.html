M.Tech. (CDS) Course Requirements

M.Tech. (CDS) students must earn a minimum of 64 credits including the project. The minimum CGPA required to continue in the M.Tech. programme is 5.0 at the end of the first year.

Coursework is split into hard core courses, soft core courses and electives. Students choose electives in consultation with their faculty advisor, and may take up to two electives from other departments.

Project

The M.Tech. project carries 25 credits and is carried out in the second year under a CDS faculty advisor.
