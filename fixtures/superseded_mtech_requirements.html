M.Tech. (CDS) Course Requirements

SUPERSEDED-FIXTURE. This is an old, superseded version of this page kept only to test version filtering.

M.Tech. (CDS) students must earn a minimum of 52 credits, of which at least 30 credits must come from DS courses. The minimum CGPA required to continue in the M.Tech. programme is 4.5 at the end of the first year.

Project

The M.Tech. project carries 24 credits and must be completed by the end of the second year. SUPERSEDED-FIXTURE.
